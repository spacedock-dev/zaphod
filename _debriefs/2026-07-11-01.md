## Decisions

- Canonical installation is primary-checkout-only; worktree testing remains
  disposable and globally isolated.
- Foreign-tab retrofit left the release path. Zaphod owns one managed tab or
  window per binding.
- `Alt Shift z` creates or focuses the managed view. `Alt /` has no layout
  effect outside it.
- Existing panes enter through explicit adoption, preserving pane and process
  identity; consent is never automated.
- CLI `Run` remains a proving harness. Production keybindings require a thin
  native controller.
- Sprint 1 contains foreground-profile repair, native CLI ownership, binding
  core, and Zellij identity feasibility.
- Production controller delivery moves to Sprint 2; pane adoption moves to
  Sprint 3.
- The senior review approved the direction with reframing and prohibited
  implementation under the original slices.
- `docs/roadmap.md` is direct first-officer planning work when explicitly
  authorized; it does not require a dispatched worker.

## Observations

- A session-existence test is not an interactive TUI test. Foreground process-
  group ownership and raw PTY input must be proved before captain drills.
- Stable tab IDs are locators, not ownership proof; binding generation, marker
  identity, and session incarnation must agree before mutation.
- The senior sprint review caught cross-task contradictions that each ideation
  packet missed in isolation.
- Context compaction recovery must rely on durable continuation state, not on
  summaries preserving prose instructions.
- The managed-view pivot removes destructive foreign-tab reconstruction and
  the upstream transactional requirement from the release critical path.
