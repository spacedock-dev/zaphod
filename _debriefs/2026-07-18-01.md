## Decisions

- Dynamic dock/sliver layout mutation is retired. A layout-owned, fixed
  28-column rail is the safest effective approach with the current Zellij API.
- `Alt /`, the `FIXED` header, and stale toggle pipes do not mutate layout.
- Native fullscreen is the supported temporary space-reclamation mechanism.
- The selected-worktree script creates a managed tab from dedicated inline KDL.
  `Alt Shift z` remains only a fixed installed-layout shortcut.
- Review findings must identify outcome versus evidence defects and their
  release scope. Authoritative review stops at the convergence cap unless the
  captain explicitly authorizes more.
